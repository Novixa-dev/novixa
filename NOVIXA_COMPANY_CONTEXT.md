## 37. Important Design Review Principle

For every major section ask:

- Why does this exist?
- What should the user understand here?
- Why this layout?
- Why this visual treatment?
- What is the primary hierarchy?
- What action should happen next?
- Does this strengthen Novixa's identity?
- Could this section belong to another company?
- Does it feel generic or AI-generated?

If a section could belong to many unrelated technology companies, strengthen its Novixa-specific identity.
